## 1. Công thức chung T–H–T' (Ch.4), mâu thuẫn (Ch.5), sức lao động và "hidden abode" (Ch.6)

### Takeaway
Các điểm của bản trước về Ch.4–6 đều ĐÚNG và nay có nguyên văn. Lưu ý: tên chương bản Anh là "The General Formula for Capital", "Contradictions in the General Formula of Capital", "The Buying and Selling of Labour-Power" (Part II). Thứ tự trong Ch.6: "hidden abode" đứng ngay *trước* câu "Eden of the innate rights of man" (Eden là mô tả cái lĩnh vực mà họ rời đi).

### Cited Findings
- Ch.4: "The exact form of this process is therefore M—C—M′, where M′ = M + ∆ M = the original sum advanced, plus an increment. This increment or excess over the original value I call 'surplus-value.' The value originally advanced, therefore, not only remains intact while in circulation, but adds to itself a surplus-value or expands itself. It is this movement that converts it into capital." — [Ch.4](https://www.marxists.org/archive/marx/works/1867-c1/ch04.htm)
- Ch.4: nhà tư bản "functions as a capitalist, that is, as capital personified and endowed with consciousness and a will … The restless never-ending process of profit-making alone is what he aims at." — [Ch.4](https://www.marxists.org/archive/marx/works/1867-c1/ch04.htm). (Ký hiệu của Marx là M—C—M′; "T–H–T'" là cách dịch Việt.)
- Ch.5: "It is therefore impossible for capital to be produced by circulation, and it is equally impossible for it to originate apart from circulation. It must have its origin both in circulation and yet not in circulation." Rồi: "Our friend, Moneybags … must buy his commodities at their value, must sell them at their value, and yet at the end of the process must withdraw more value from circulation than he threw into it at starting… These are the conditions of the problem. Hic Rhodus, hic salta!" — [Ch.5](https://www.marxists.org/archive/marx/works/1867-c1/ch05.htm). Ghi chú trước viết "arise" ("không thể xuất hiện từ lưu thông…"): bản này dùng "originate", không phải "arise". Trích cho đúng.
- Ch.6, hai điều kiện: "the owner of money must meet in the market with the free labourer, free in the double sense, that as a free man he can dispose of his labour-power as his own commodity, and that on the other hand he has no other commodity for sale, is short of everything necessary for the realisation of his labour-power." — [Ch.6](https://www.marxists.org/archive/marx/works/1867-c1/ch06.htm). Cụm "free in the double sense" ĐÚNG và có nguyên văn.
- Ch.6, giá trị sức lao động gồm cả con cái: "the sum of the means of subsistence necessary for the production of labour-power must include the means necessary for the labourer's substitutes, i.e., his children"; và "In contradistinction therefore to the case of other commodities, there enters into the determination of the value of labour-power a historical and moral element." — [Ch.6](https://www.marxists.org/archive/marx/works/1867-c1/ch06.htm). (Câu trước đó: nhu cầu "are themselves the product of historical development, and depend therefore to a great extent on the degree of civilisation of a country".)
- Ch.6, cuối chương: "we therefore take leave for a time of this noisy sphere … and follow them both into the hidden abode of production, on whose threshold there stares us in the face 'No admittance except on business.' Here we shall see, not only how capital produces, but how capital is produced. We shall at last force the secret of profit making. This sphere that we are deserting … is in fact a very Eden of the innate rights of man. There alone rule Freedom, Equality, Property and Bentham." — [Ch.6](https://www.marxists.org/archive/marx/works/1867-c1/ch06.htm). Bản trước dịch "Eden của những quyền tự nhiên"; nguyên văn là "innate rights of man".

### Inferences
- Bản tóm lược trước ở nhóm này đúng nội dung; chỉ cần chỉnh nhỏ: (a) "arise" → "originate" khi trích, (b) tên Ch.6 là "mua và bán sức lao động".

### Gaps
- Không còn gap đáng kể cho nhóm này.

## 2. Ch.7–9: ví dụ sợi, tư bản bất biến/khả biến, tỷ suất GTTD

### Takeaway
Cả hai bộ số ĐÚNG. Ví dụ sợi (10 lb bông, 10s, 2s, 3s, 6/12 giờ) ở **Ch.7 §2**; ví dụ £500 = £410c + £90v ở **Ch.9 §1**. Điểm cần sửa: bản trước ghi 18% là "tự tính" nhưng chính Marx tự tính 18% ở Ch.9 để phê phán cách tính "thông thường".

### Cited Findings
- Ch.7 §2 (nằm trong chương "The Labour-Process and the Process of Producing Surplus-Value"; bản trước gọi chương này là "quá trình làm tăng giá trị", nay chuẩn hơn là "quá trình sản xuất giá trị thặng dư"): "suppose in this case 10 lbs. of cotton … bought it at its full value, say of ten shillings … the wear and tear of the spindle … amounts to the value of 2s." — [Ch.7](https://www.marxists.org/archive/marx/works/1867-c1/ch07.htm).
- Ch.7: "We assumed … that the value of a day's labour-power is three shillings, and that six hours' labour is incorporated in that sum … in six hours he will convert 10 lbs. of cotton into 10 lbs. of yarn … fifteen shillings is an adequate price for the 10 lbs. of yarn, or the price of one pound is eighteenpence. Our capitalist stares in astonishment. The value of the product is exactly equal to the value of the capital advanced." — [Ch.7](https://www.marxists.org/archive/marx/works/1867-c1/ch07.htm).
- Ch.7: "so now, 20 lbs. of cotton will absorb 12 hours' labour and be changed into 20 lbs. of yarn … the labour of five days … Expressed in gold … thirty shillings … the sum of the values of the commodities that entered into the process amounts to 27 shillings. The value of the yarn is 30 shillings … 27 shillings have been transformed into 30 shillings; a surplus-value of 3 shillings has been created. The trick has at last succeeded; money has been converted into capital. Every condition of the problem is satisfied, while the laws that regulate the exchange of commodities, have been in no way violated." — [Ch.7](https://www.marxists.org/archive/marx/works/1867-c1/ch07.htm). Khớp hoàn toàn với bản trước (20s + 4s + 3s = 27; 30s; GTTD 3s). Marx còn nói: "the latter is nothing but the continuation of the former beyond a definite point".
- Ch.8: "I therefore call it the constant part of capital, or, more shortly, constant capital … I therefore call it the variable part of capital, or, shortly, variable capital." (variable: "It both reproduces the equivalent of its own value, and also produces an excess, a surplus-value") — [Ch.8](https://www.marxists.org/archive/marx/works/1867-c1/ch08.htm).
- Ch.9 §1: "C = c + v: for example, if £500 is the capital advanced, its components may be such that the £500 = £410 const. + £90 var. … (£410 const. + £90 var.) + £90 surpl. The original capital has now changed from C to C′, from £500 to £590." Chi tiết thành phần c: "£312 value of raw material, £44 value of auxiliary material, and £54 value of the machinery worn away … the total value of the machinery employed is £1,054". — [Ch.9](https://www.marxists.org/archive/marx/works/1867-c1/ch09.htm).
- Ch.9: "The rate of surplus-value is therefore an exact expression for the degree of exploitation of labour-power by capital, or of the labourer by the capitalist." Marx tự tính: "according to the usual way of reckoning, get as the rate of surplus-value (generally confounded with rate of profits) 18% … But in truth, the rate of surplus-value is not equal to s/C or s/(c+v), but to s/v: thus it is not 90/500 but 90/90 or 100%, which is more than five times the apparent degree of exploitation." — [Ch.9](https://www.marxists.org/archive/marx/works/1867-c1/ch09.htm).
- Ch.9 §3 "Senior's 'Last Hour'": Marx trích bản Senior (Letters on the Factory Act, 1837): nhà máy dùng người dưới 18 tuổi "can be worked more than 11½ hours a day, that is, 12 hours for 5 days in the week, and nine on Saturday", và Senior khẳng định "the whole net profit is derived from the last hour" (ví dụ vốn £100,000: £80,000 nhà máy máy móc, £20,000 nguyên liệu và tiền công) — [Ch.9](https://www.marxists.org/archive/marx/works/1867-c1/ch09.htm). Con số 11½ giờ trong bản trước ĐÚNG.
- Ch.9 §1 tên: "The Degree of Exploitation of Labour-Power"; §2 "The Representation of the Components of the Value of the Product…"; §4 "Surplus-Produce".

### Inferences
- Sửa nhãn: ví dụ sợi = Ch.7 §2 (bản trước gộp "Ch.7–8"); p' = 18% (90/500) không còn là "tự tính" mà là con số Marx tự nêu (Ch.9 §1); tuy nhiên ghi chú "p' tăng/giảm khi c/v = 9" vẫn là minh họa tự tính.
- Marx gọi 90/500 là "rate of surplus-value (generally confounded with rate of profits)", tức Quyển I đã nhắc tỷ suất lợi nhuận kiểu thông thường để bác bỏ, chứ không phải chưa hề nhắc như bản trước nói ("chưa nói tỷ suất lợi nhuận").

### Gaps
- Chưa đọc từng chữ phần giữa Ch.8 (khấu hao, ví dụ giá bông tăng/giảm) và các §2, §4 Ch.9.

## 3. Ngày lao động và Factory Acts (Ch.10), GTTD tương đối/siêu ngạch (Ch.12), Ch.13–15, Ch.16–18

### Takeaway
Tìm thấy nguyên văn "vampire" (3 lần) và xác định đúng mục. Bản trước sai mục ở "Ch.10 §3 – phong trào 8 giờ": thực tế ở **§7**; Walkley ở **§3**; các Factory Acts ở **§6**. Danh sách năm Factory Acts của bản trước: 1833, 1844, 1847, 1850 ĐÚNG (có trong §6); **1867** là Factory Acts Extension Act, nằm ở **Ch.15 §9**, không ở Ch.10 §6.

### Cited Findings
- Ch.10 §1: "Capital is dead labour, that, vampire-like, only lives by sucking living labour, and lives the more, the more labour it sucks." — [Ch.10](https://www.marxists.org/archive/marx/works/1867-c1/ch10.htm). Ngay trước đó: "capital has one single life impulse, the tendency to create value and surplus-value…". Ngay sau §1: "There is here, therefore, an antinomy, right against right, both equally bearing the seal of the law of exchanges. Between equal rights force decides." (cũng §1).
- Ch.10 §4: "It quenches only in a slight degree the vampire thirst for the living blood of labour." Ch.10 §7 (câu cuối chương): "the vampire will not lose its hold on him 'so long as there is a muscle, a nerve, a drop of blood to be exploited'" và "the modest Magna Charta of a legally limited working-day". Bản "werewolf" trong kết quả tìm kiếm phiên trước chưa kiểm lại ở đây.
- Ch.10 §3: Mary Anne Walkley: "In the last week of June, 1863 … 'Death from simple over-work.' It dealt with the death of the milliner, Mary Anne Walkley, 20 years of age … This girl worked, on an average, 16½ hours, during the season often 30 hours". Năm 1863 ĐÚNG; mục §3 (không phải §6). Thợ làm ren Nottingham: có trong Ch.10 (§2/§3, chưa xác định mục chính xác).
- Ch.10 §6 (tiêu đề: "The English Factory Acts, 1833 to 1864"): "A normal working-day for modern industry only dates from the Factory Act of 1833"; Luật 1833: 15 giờ từ 5.30 đến 8.30 tối; "the Act of 1833 … remained unaltered until June, 1844"; "The new Factory Act of June 8th, 1847, enacted that on July 1st, 1847, there should be a preliminary shortening of the working-day for 'young persons' (from 13 to 18), and all females to 11 hours, but that on May 1st, 1848, there should be a definite limitation of the working-day to 10 hours."; Đạo luật 1850 (thỏa hiệp, relay system) cũng ở §6. Ch.10 §2 chú thích: "The Factory Act of 1850 now in force (1867) allows … 10 hours …" — [Ch.10](https://www.marxists.org/archive/marx/works/1867-c1/ch10.htm).
- Ch.10 §7: "The first fruit of the Civil War was the eight hours' agitation … The General Congress of labour at Baltimore (August 16th, 1866) declared: 'The first and great necessity … is the passing of a law by which eight hours shall be the normal working-day in all States of the American Union.'" — cùng Ch.10. **Do đó "phong trào 8 giờ" = Ch.10 §7, không phải §3.**
- Ch.15 §9: "On the 15th August, 1867, the Factory Acts Extension Act, and on the 21st August, the Workshops' Regulation Act received the Royal Assent" — [Ch.15](https://www.marxists.org/archive/marx/works/1867-c1/ch15.htm).
- Ch.11 tên: "Rate and Mass of Surplus Value".
- Ch.12 (Part IV, "The Concept of Relative Surplus Value"): tên GTTD tương đối: "…I call relative surplus-value"; siêu ngạch: "he sells it for threepence above its individual value, and thus realises an extra surplus-value of threepence." "The general rate of surplus-value is, therefore, ultimately affected by the whole process, only when the increase in the productiveness of labour, has seized upon those branches of production … that form part of the necessary means of subsistence"; động cơ cá nhân: "he by no means necessarily aims at reducing the value of labour-power … But it is only in so far as he ultimately contributes to this result, that he assists in raising the general rate of surplus-value"; cạnh tranh: "this same law, acting as a coercive law of competition, forces his competitors to adopt the new method." — [Ch.12](https://www.marxists.org/archive/marx/works/1867-c1/ch12.htm). Bản trước đúng.
- Ch.13: "A greater number of labourers working together … under the mastership of one capitalist, constitutes, both historically and logically, the starting-point of capitalist production." — [Ch.13](https://www.marxists.org/archive/marx/works/1867-c1/ch13.htm). Ch.14 §3 "The two fundamental forms of manufacture": "The organisation of manufacture has two fundamental forms…"; §2 "converts the labourer into a crippled monstrosity"; "anarchy in the social division of labour and despotism in that of the workshop are mutual conditions the one of the other" — [Ch.14](https://www.marxists.org/archive/marx/works/1867-c1/ch14.htm). Bản trước đúng ý; mục §2/§4/§5 theo tiêu đề: §1 Two-fold Origin, §2 Detail Labourer, §3 Two Fundamental Forms, §4 Division of Labour in Manufacture and in Society, §5 Capitalistic Character.
- Ch.15 (10 mục: §1 Development of Machinery … §9 Factory Acts, §10 Modern Industry and Agriculture): "The labour of women and children was, therefore, the first thing sought for by capitalists who used machinery"; "the economic paradox, that the most powerful instrument for shortening labour-time, becomes the most unfailing means for placing every moment of the labourer's time and that of his family, at the disposal of the capitalist"; Luddite: "The enormous destruction of machinery … known as the Luddite movement" (§5) — [Ch.15](https://www.marxists.org/archive/marx/works/1867-c1/ch15.htm).
- Ch.16: "The production of absolute surplus-value turns exclusively upon the length of the working-day; the production of relative surplus-value, revolutionises out and out the technical processes of labour, and the composition of society … on the foundation afforded by the formal subjection of labour to capital. In the course of this development, the formal subjection is replaced by the real subjection of labour to capital." — [Ch.16](https://www.marxists.org/archive/marx/works/1867-c1/ch16.htm). **Chỉnh bản trước:** khái niệm "formal/real subjection" không chỉ có ở bản thảo "Results" mà đã nằm ngay trong Ch.16.
- Ch.17 (4 mục: năng suất biến thiên; ngày lao động không đổi; …; biến thiên đồng thời) và Ch.18 "Various Formula for the Rate of Surplus-Value" — đã xác nhận tên/cấu trúc.

### Inferences
- Danh sách năm của bản trước chỉ cần thêm: 1864 (cuối khoảng "1833 to 1864" trong tiêu đề §6) và chuyển 1867 sang Ch.15.

### Gaps
- Chưa đọc từng chữ phần thống kê/ví dụ Ch.10 §2–§5 (Nottingham, thợ gốm…), Ch.14 §1–§3, Ch.15 §1–§8; mục §10 Ch.15 (nông nghiệp) chỉ xác nhận qua tiêu đề.

## 4. Tiền công (Ch.19–22), tích lũy (Ch.23–25), tích lũy nguyên thủy (Ch.26–33)

### Takeaway
Bản trước đúng phần lớn. Hai chỉnh: (i) sai giới tính "Công tước Sutherland" → **Duchess of Sutherland** (Ch.27); (ii) câu "máu và bùn" ở Ch.31 là lời Marx, kèm dẫn Augier ở thân bài; chú thích số 15 gắn vào câu đó là đoạn trích T. J. Dunning (về lợi nhuận 300%), nên "trích Dunning" là gán nhầm nếu hiểu là câu của Dunning. "Organic composition" có nguyên văn định nghĩa ở Ch.25 §1.

### Cited Findings
- Ch.19: "The wage form thus extinguishes every trace of the division of the working-day into necessary labour and surplus-labour, into paid and unpaid labour. All labour appears as paid labour. In the corvée, the labour of the worker for himself, and his compulsory labour for his lord, differ in space and time in the clearest possible way." Và "As the value of labour is only an irrational expression for the value of labour-power…" — [Ch.19](https://www.marxists.org/archive/marx/works/1867-c1/ch19.htm).
- Ch.20: giá một giờ: "If … the habitual working-day is 10 hours and the daily value of the labour-power 3s., the price of the working-hour is 3 3/5d. It falls to 3d. as soon as the working-day rises to 12 hours" — [Ch.20](https://www.marxists.org/archive/marx/works/1867-c1/ch20.htm). Ch.21 "Piece-Wages": "the difference of form in the payment of wages alters in no way their essential nature, although the one form may be more favorable to the development of capitalist production than the other" — [Ch.21](https://www.marxists.org/archive/marx/works/1867-c1/ch21.htm). Bản trước nói tiền công theo sản phẩm là hình thức "thích hợp nhất": Marx viết "more favorable to the development of capitalist production" (cần dịch đúng mức độ). Ch.22: "a certain average intensity of labour below which the labour … does not reckon as labour of normal quality" — [Ch.22](https://www.marxists.org/archive/marx/works/1867-c1/ch22.htm).
- Ch.23 "Simple Reproduction": "Capitalist production, therefore, under its aspect of a continuous connected process, of a process of reproduction, produces not only commodities, not only surplus-value, but it also produces and reproduces the capitalist relation; on the one side the capitalist, on the other the wage labourer." — [Ch.23](https://www.marxists.org/archive/marx/works/1867-c1/ch23.htm).
- Ch.24 §1: "the laws of appropriation or of private property, laws that are based on the production and circulation of commodities, become by their own inner and inexorable dialectic changed into their very opposite." Ch.24 §3 (tiêu đề: "Separation of Surplus-Value into Capital and Revenue. The Abstinence Theory"): "Accumulate, accumulate! That is Moses and the prophets! … Accumulation for accumulation's sake, production for production's sake" — [Ch.24](https://www.marxists.org/archive/marx/works/1867-c1/ch24.htm). Mục §3, §4, §5 của bản trước đúng (§4 các yếu tố quyết định quy mô tích lũy; §5 "The So-Called Labour Fund").
- Ch.25 §1: "I call the value composition of capital, in so far as it is determined by its technical composition and mirrors the changes of the latter, the organic composition of capital. Wherever I refer to the composition of capital, without further qualification, its organic composition is always understood." Tiêu đề §1: "The Increased Demand for Labour-Power that Accompanies Accumulation, the Composition of Capital Remaining the Same". Sau đó: "the proportion of constant to variable capital … originally say 1:1, it now becomes successively 2:1, 3:1, 4:1, 5:1, 7:1, &c." — [Ch.25](https://www.marxists.org/archive/marx/works/1867-c1/ch25.htm). (Lưu ý: nhận định phiên trước "tìm kiếm không xác nhận organic composition ở Ch.25" nay được sửa: có ở §1.)
- Ch.25 §3 ("Progressive Production of a Relative Surplus Population or Industrial Reserve Army"): "a surplus labouring population is a necessary product of accumulation … this surplus population becomes, conversely, the lever of capitalistic accumulation, nay, a condition of existence of the capitalist mode of production. It forms a disposable industrial reserve army, that belongs to capital quite as absolutely as if the latter had bred it at its own cost."
- Ch.25 §4 ("Different Forms of the Relative Surplus Population. The General Law of Capitalist Accumulation"): "it has always three forms, the floating, the latent, the stagnant"; "The third category … the stagnant, forms a part of the active labour army, but with extremely irregular employment. Hence it furnishes to capital an inexhaustible reservoir of disposable labour power."; "The more extensive, finally, the lazarus layers of the working class, and the industrial reserve army, the greater is official pauperism. This is the absolute general law of capitalist accumulation. Like all other laws it is modified in its working by many circumstances…"; "Accumulation of wealth at one pole is, therefore, at the same time accumulation of misery, agony of toil slavery, ignorance, brutality, mental degradation, at the opposite pole". Chu kỳ: "a decennial cycle … depends on the constant formation, the greater or less absorption, and the re-formation of the industrial reserve army". — [Ch.25](https://www.marxists.org/archive/marx/works/1867-c1/ch25.htm). Ghi chú trước nói "§4" cho câu kết; ĐÚNG. Lưu ý văn bản cho "accumulation of misery" (không phải "bần cùng hóa tuyệt đối" như thuật ngữ về sau).
- Ch.26 ("The Secret of Primitive Accumulation"): "accumulation of capital presupposes surplus-value; surplus-value presupposes capitalistic production … The whole movement, therefore, seems to turn in a vicious circle … primitive accumulation … an accumulation not the result of the capitalistic mode of production, but its starting point. This primitive accumulation plays in Political Economy about the same part as original sin in theology." "In actual history it is notorious that conquest, enslavement, robbery, murder, briefly force, play the great part." — [Ch.26](https://www.marxists.org/archive/marx/works/1867-c1/ch26.htm). Bản trước đúng.
- Ch.27: "the 'clearing' made by the Duchess of Sutherland" (1814–1820) — [Ch.27](https://www.marxists.org/archive/marx/works/1867-c1/ch27.htm). **Sửa:** Nữ Công tước (Duchess), không phải Công tước (Duke).
- Ch.28 tên: "Bloody Legislation against the Expropriated, from the End of the 15th Century. Forcing down of Wages by Acts of Parliament"; Ch.29 "Genesis of the Capitalist Farmer"; Ch.30 "Reaction of the Agricultural Revolution on Industry. Creation of the Home-Market for Industrial Capital"; Ch.31 "Genesis of the Industrial Capitalist" (bản trước tóm đúng).
- Ch.31: "If money, according to Augier, 'comes into the world with a congenital blood-stain on one cheek,' capital comes dripping from head to foot, from every pore, with blood and dirt." Chú thích 15 gắn vào câu đó là: "Capital eschews no profit … 100 per cent. will make it ready to trample on all human laws; 300 per cent., and there is not a crime at which it will scruple… (T. J. Dunning, l. c., pp. 35, 36.)" — [Ch.31](https://www.marxists.org/archive/marx/works/1867-c1/ch31.htm). **Sửa:** câu "máu và bùn" là Marx; Dunning chỉ là nguồn của chú thích kèm (lợi nhuận 300%); Augier (Marie Angier, *Du Crédit Public*) là nguồn của "blood-stain on one cheek".
- Ch.32 ("Historical Tendency of Capitalist Accumulation"): "Centralisation of the means of production and socialisation of labour at last reach a point where they become incompatible with their capitalist integument. This integument is burst asunder. The knell of capitalist private property sounds. The expropriators are expropriated." và "It is the negation of negation. This does not re-establish private property for the producer, but gives him individual property based on the acquisition of the capitalist era: i.e., on cooperation and the possession in common of the land and of the means of production." — [Ch.32](https://www.marxists.org/archive/marx/works/1867-c1/ch32.htm). Bản trước đúng; nhưng "phủ định của phủ định" dẫn đúng chỗ này, và lưu ý: Marx KHÔNG nói "tái lập tư hữu cho người sản xuất" mà "individual property" dựa trên hiệp tác và sở hữu chung.
- Ch.33 ("The Modern Theory of Colonisation"): "He discovered that capital is not a thing, but a social relation between persons, established by the instrumentality of things." (Wakefield; ví dụ Mr. Peel ở Swan River, £50,000 và 300 người) — [Ch.33](https://www.marxists.org/archive/marx/works/1867-c1/ch33.htm). Bản trước đúng ý nhưng nguyên văn là Marx nói Wakefield *đã khám phá* điều này.

### Inferences
- Chuỗi liên kết bản trước đề xuất (Ch.19 → 23–24 → 25 → 26–33 → hai điều kiện "tự do" ở Ch.6) được văn bản ủng hộ: Ch.26 nêu "starting point", Ch.33 nêu thiếu "wage-worker" ở thuộc địa.

### Gaps
- Chưa đọc từng chữ Ch.22, 28–30, các chú thích Ch.20–21.

## 5. Cách đánh số chương giữa các bản dịch

### Takeaway
Bản marxists.org (Moore–Aveling, Engels biên tập, có chỉnh theo bản Đức lần 4) đánh số **Ch.1–33** theo 8 Part: Part II = Ch.4–6; III = Ch.7–11; IV = Ch.12–15; V = Ch.16–18; VI = Ch.19–22; VII = Ch.23–25; VIII = Ch.26–33 (đọc trực tiếp từ index.htm). Các điều sau về bản Đức/Fowkes/Việt KHÔNG kiểm được trong phiên này.

### Cited Findings
- Cấu trúc/tên chương như trên — [index](https://www.marxists.org/archive/marx/works/1867-c1/index.htm). Bản trước chia Part đúng.
- **Chưa kiểm** (không có trong trang): bản Đức đánh số 1–25 (Part VII Ch.24 "Die sogenannte ursprüngliche Akkumulation" chứa 7 mục, gộp Ch.26–33 Anh; "expropriators are expropriated" = Ch.24 §7 Đức); bản Đức Ch.5 = Ch.7 Anh, Ch.8 = Ch.10 (ngày lao động), Ch.23 = Ch.25. Bản Việt (NXB Sự thật, Toàn tập t.23) nhiều khả năng theo cách đánh số Đức; Penguin/Fowkes theo cách đánh số 1–33 giống Moore–Aveling. Phiên trước, nguồn thứ cấp (Balibar) chỉ xác nhận "Ch.24 §7 Đức = Ch.32 Anh".

### Inferences
- Câu trong bản trước "bản Anh mới của Fowkes đánh số khác … Phần VIII là Ch.26–33" tự mâu thuẫn; nên bỏ, và chỉ nói: trích Ch. theo Moore–Aveling 1–33; nếu đối chiếu bản Việt/Đức thì cần quy đổi và phải mở bản đó để kiểm.

### Gaps
- Không có bản Đức/Fowkes/Việt để kiểm; phần "bản Việt theo bản Đức" vẫn là phỏng đoán.

## 6. Tổng hợp các điểm bản tóm lược trước SAI hoặc cần chỉnh (đã kiểm với văn bản)

1. **Ví dụ sợi** gắn nhãn Ch.7–8: đúng là **Ch.7 §2**; Ch.8 chỉ có định nghĩa c/v. Con số đều đúng.

3. **"Ch.10 §3 – phong trào 8 giờ"**: SAI mục → **§7**. **Walkley 1863** ở **§3** (đúng năm). **Factory Acts 1833/1844/1847/1850** đúng (§6); **1867** là Ch.15 §9.
